Case Study  ·  01 / Data + ML

STOCK 5: AI Stock Recommendation System

A hybrid LSTM + ARIMA engine that turns raw market data into ranked, risk-scored stock recommendations.

PythonLSTMARIMA PandasNumPyReact Node.jsYahoo Finance

The Problem

Most retail-facing stock tools show raw price history and leave the interpretation to the user. I wanted to build something that goes a step further: take live market data and turn it into a ranked, explainable shortlist, complete with a confidence and risk score, so the output reads like an analyst's recommendation rather than a raw chart.

Why a Hybrid Model

Pure LSTM models are good at learning nonlinear patterns in sequential price data but can drift on longer horizons. ARIMA is the opposite: strong at capturing linear trend and seasonality but blind to the nonlinear structure LSTM picks up. Rather than pick one, STOCK 5 runs both and blends their outputs, using ARIMA to correct and stabilize the LSTM's forecast instead of treating either model as the sole source of truth.

Market data is pulled through the Yahoo Finance API, cleaned and windowed with Pandas and NumPy, then passed through the ensemble model. The Node.js backend exposes the scored output to a React frontend, which renders the top 5 recommendations, daily gainers and losers, and per-stock risk and confidence scores.

Challenges & Trade-offs

  • Blending two very different models. LSTM and ARIMA output forecasts on different scales and with different error characteristics, so combining them cleanly took real tuning rather than a naive average.
  • Turning a forecast into a decision. A predicted price isn't a recommendation. Converting raw predictions into a risk score and a confidence score meant defining what "confidence" should actually mean for a retail user, not just a model metric.
  • Keeping it real-time. Market data changes constantly, so the pipeline had to refresh, re-score, and re-rank without becoming slow or inconsistent on the frontend.

What It Does Today

  • Ranks and surfaces the top 5 stock recommendations from live market data.
  • Scores each recommendation for risk and confidence, alongside a predicted return.
  • Surfaces daily gainers and losers for quick portfolio context.
  • Presents everything through a React dashboard backed by a Node.js API.
